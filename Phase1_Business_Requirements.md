# Phase 1: Understanding Business Requirements

## 🎯 Objective
- Understand how customer support teams currently manage large volumes of service requests.
- Identify the challenges involved in manual ticket prioritization and assignment.
- Build an intelligent system using Salesforce and Agentforce that automatically analyzes ticket details, determines priority levels, and assigns them to the right support agents.
- Aim to reduce response time, improve customer satisfaction, minimize manual effort, and ensure efficient handling of critical issues.

## 🛠️ Approach
- Gather requirements from key stakeholders (support agents, managers, and customers).
- Study how tickets are currently created, prioritized, and assigned manually.
- Identify major challenges such as delays in resolving urgent issues, inconsistent prioritization, and uneven workload distribution.
- Analyze sample ticket data to identify commonly used keywords that indicate urgency (e.g., "urgent", "not working", "failure").
- Conduct research using Salesforce Trailhead and documentation to design a scalable solution.
- Plan the system architecture using Salesforce components like custom objects, flows, and Agentforce for AI-based automation.

## 📋 Key Business Requirements Identified
- Centralized System: Provide a centralized system to create, store, and manage all customer support tickets.
- AI Priority Classification: Automatically classify ticket priority into High, Medium, or Low based on the issue description.
- Auto-Assignment: Enable automated assignment of tickets to the appropriate support agents.
- Agentforce Integration: Use Agentforce AI to analyze ticket details and trigger automation.
- Task Creation: Automatically create tasks for high-priority tickets to ensure immediate attention.
- Manager Visibility: Provide visibility to managers for monitoring ticket status, priority, and agent workload.
- Performance Tracking: Generate reports and dashboards for tracking performance and efficiency.
- Security: Ensure secure access with role-based permissions for agents and managers.
- Customer Experience: Improve overall response time and customer experience.
